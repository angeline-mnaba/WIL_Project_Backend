Operations

Move through the atelier workflow from first client contact to delivery and support.

Create Customer & Order Invite a client and open a new furniture order. Quotations Quick quotes for prospects, link them to clients later. Receipts Receipts for payments received, link them to clients later. Staff Details Set the name, phone and email shown on quotations. Production Board See what is in production, by due date. Order Management Update production stages, order items, payments, and balances. Job Cards Create and sort production spec sheets by estimated completion date. Delivery Bookings Confirm, decline, and complete requested delivery dates. Support Requests Review client questions and respond from the staff portal. Contact Inquiries Review public website inquiries and update their follow-up status.
